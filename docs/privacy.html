Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 4, 2026

Don’t Smoke is designed with privacy in mind. The app helps you track your smoke-free progress, personal motivation, savings, craving times, and reminders.

Information Stored by the App

Don’t Smoke may store the following information on your device:

  • Your quit date and time
  • Approximate cigarettes smoked per day
  • Cigarette or pack cost
  • Your selected currency
  • Your personal reason for quitting
  • Optional personal notes
  • Optional motivation photo
  • Selected craving triggers and reminder times
  • App preferences and settings

How Your Data Is Used

This information is used only to provide app features such as:

  • Calculating smoke-free time
  • Estimating cigarettes avoided
  • Calculating money saved
  • Displaying your personal motivation
  • Providing craving support
  • Scheduling local reminders

Data Storage

Your personal quit-smoking data is stored locally on your device unless otherwise stated in a future version of the app.

Don’t Smoke does not currently require an account or transmit your quit progress to our servers.

Photos

If you choose to add a motivation photo, the photo is used only within the app to support your personal “My Why” experience.

Don’t Smoke does not upload your selected photo to our servers.

Notifications

If you enable craving reminders, Don’t Smoke uses Apple’s local notification system to schedule reminders on your device.

You can disable notifications at any time in the app or in iOS Settings.

Analytics and Tracking

Don’t Smoke does not currently use third-party advertising trackers or sell your personal data.

Third-Party Services

The current version of Don’t Smoke does not rely on third-party services for storing your personal quit-smoking information.

Children’s Privacy

Don’t Smoke is not specifically directed toward children under the age required by applicable law to independently use digital services.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated when the app adds new features or services. Any updates will be published on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.
