Common Questions

How do I change my quit date?

Open the Me tab in the app and update your quit information from settings.

Why am I not receiving craving reminders?

Make sure notifications are enabled for Don’t Smoke in your iPhone Settings.

How do I change my currency?

Open the Me tab and update your preferred currency from Settings.

How do I update My Why?

Open the Me tab and edit your personal reason, message, or photo.
